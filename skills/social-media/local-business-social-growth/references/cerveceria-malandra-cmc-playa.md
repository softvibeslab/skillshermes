# Session Reference: Cervecería Malandra + Colectivo Mexicano Cervecero, Playa del Carmen

Use as an example of a local-business Instagram/competitor audit for a cervecería/bar.

## Public Instagram metadata: @cerveceria.malandra

Fetched from `https://www.instagram.com/cerveceria.malandra/` public meta tags.

- Display name: Malandra
- Handle: @cerveceria.malandra
- Visible metrics: 76 followers, 93 following, 0 posts
- Bio: `Cervecería Artesanal Est. 2025\n📍 Playa del Carmen, México\nNo es para todos, es para ti 🍻🍻`
- Interpretation: pre-launch or inactive account; has brand promise but little commercial proof.

## Visual/logo notes

- Style: vintage, artesanal, rebelde/canalla; beige, red, black.
- Strength: “MALANDRA” is memorable and legible enough as main wordmark.
- Weakness: small secondary text and detailed illustration lose clarity at Instagram avatar size.
- Recommendation: create simplified avatar version with only the wordmark and/or a clear icon; avoid tiny slogans in the circular crop.

## Business reading

For a new Playa del Carmen cervecería account:
- Strengths: clear location, distinctive name, attitude-driven slogan, handmade/craft positioning.
- Gaps: no posts, no highlights, no CTA, no WhatsApp/reservation link, no address/hours/menu, no proof of product or ambience.
- First content should establish legitimacy: intro post, beer pour reel, identity/slogan post, location reel, “qué somos / cuándo abrimos / dónde estamos” carousel, highlights for menú/ubicación/horarios/cervezas/eventos/reservas.
