# Playa Parties session pattern

This reference captures the reusable pattern from analyzing a WhatsApp export named “Playa Parties”.

## Inputs observed
- WhatsApp ZIP containing `_chat.txt`.
- Chat period: roughly one year.
- Community: nightlife/events in Playa del Carmen/Riviera Maya.
- Mix of Spanish/English promotional posts.

## High-signal extraction fields
- Sender frequency and event-post frequency.
- Latest post date anchored to transcript max date.
- Prices: `$100`, `120 MXN`, `100 pesos`, `$5 USD`, etc.
- Links/domains: Google Maps, Instagram, Logintkt, WhatsApp groups, Stripe, Eventbrite, Facebook, TikTok.
- Contacts: `wa.me`, phone-like strings, Instagram handles and `@mentions`.
- Sample event text, but truncated to avoid dumping raw chat.

## Winning monetization framing
Position as a curated daily/weekly discovery product, not as another noisy group:

- Name examples: “Qué hacer hoy en Playa”, “Playa Party Radar”, “Playa Tonight”.
- Audience: tourists, expats, locals looking for plans.
- Supply: promoters, dance schools, venues, wellness hosts.

## Best first product
- WhatsApp/Telegram channel + first calendar post.
- Google Form/DM intake for promoters.
- No app until demand is validated.

## Founder offer
- First featured post free.
- Then 150–300 MXN per featured event, or 10–20% commission per booking/ticket.
- Weekly package: 800–1,500 MXN for 3 featured posts + daily calendar inclusion.

## Outreach message
Hola! Estoy lanzando un calendario bilingüe curado de eventos en Playa: *Qué hacer hoy en Playa*.

Vi que publicas eventos seguido y quiero incluirte. Esta semana estoy ofreciendo *1 destacado gratis* para medir respuesta.

Si te mando gente, podemos trabajar con:
- destacado por evento: *200 MXN*, o
- comisión por cada reserva/ticket que llegue de mi canal.

¿Quieres que publique tu próximo evento con link/WhatsApp de reserva?

## Execution guardrail
Before sending outreach, ask for explicit approval and suggest a limited batch (e.g. first 10) to avoid spam.
