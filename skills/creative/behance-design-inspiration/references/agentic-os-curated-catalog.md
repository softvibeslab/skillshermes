## Current taste hypothesis

Derived from el operador's selections, not from unverified visuals:

1. AI should feel like a system of capable actors, not one anonymous chatbot.
2. Chat is a control surface, but dashboards, workflows, history, and analytics must remain visible.
3. Agentic OS needs both consumer-mobile simplicity and operational SaaS depth.
4. Voice, content creation, CRM, and automation are expected parts of one ecosystem.
5. Trust patterns—status, safety, review, recovery, and human control—matter as much as futuristic styling.

Update this hypothesis only after visual evidence confirms or contradicts it.
